Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched fast, throughout Minot and the surrounding area.
