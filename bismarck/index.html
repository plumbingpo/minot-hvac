Keeping Your Bismarck HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement.
